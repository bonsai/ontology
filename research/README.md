# BONSAI Ontology Research

This directory contains research plans, papers, experiments, benchmarks, and empirical validation of the ontology theory.

## Research boundary

Research asks whether the theoretical model works in practice.

```text
Theory
  ↓
Hypothesis
  ↓
Experiment
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
Evaluation
  ↓
Theory revision
```

## Current research

- Tag-set matching
- Human tag matrix matching
- Cross-domain semantic discovery
- Relation-state modeling
- Cluster discovery
- Capability extraction
- repo2agent
- Evidence feedback
- Self-organization

## Theory vs Research

- `theory/` = concepts, definitions, models, principles
- `research/` = hypotheses, experiments, papers, measurements, evidence

The research layer must not silently become canonical ontology meaning. Validated results may inform theory, while declared ontology remains explicit.
